How to play

Identify every pathogen in your dish. The fewer clues you need, the more points you keep.

  1. 1

    Fill the dish

    Pick a dish size, then tap bacteria, viruses, fungi or parasites to fill it. Each one is a mystery to solve.

  2. 2

    Inoculate

    When your dish is ready, hit the Inoculate bar to start testing.

  3. 3

    Read the clues

    Each pathogen describes itself, from hard clues to easy ones. The first two are free.

  4. 4

    Name it

    Type the name, or pick from four in Easy mode, before the timer runs out. Small typos are fine. Answer fast for bonus points.
